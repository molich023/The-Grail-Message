The Author

Abd-ru-shin (Oskar Ernst Bernhardt, 1875–1941) brought the Grail Message to humanity as a beacon of clarity. His work is not a new religion, but a path for the individual to find the way to the Truth through personal conviction and logic.

Written between 1923 and 1938, the Message remains the definitive guide for those seeking to understand the Laws of the Universe and their own place within Creation.
