About the Author: Abd-ru-shin

Abd-ru-shin (Oskar Ernst Bernhardt, 1875–1941) is the author of In the Light of Truth – The Grail Message. His name translates to "Servant of the Light," reflecting his mission to bring clarity to the great problems of human existence.
